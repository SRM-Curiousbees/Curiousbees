01 · Problem Statement

The Fragmented Academic Ecosystem

Current university research operations suffer from unorganized communication channels, siloed departments, and lack of real-time governance.

❌
Fragmented Communication
Supervisory exchanges scattered across WhatsApp, unorganized Gmail threads, and physical meetings without institutional records.
High Risk of Data Loss
⏳
Opaque Milestone Progress
Scholar doctoral milestones (literature review, methodology, thesis drafts) lack structured progress tracking and feedback loops.
Delayed Thesis Defense
📊
Audit & NIRF Blindspots
University leadership lacks real-time dashboards for institutional publications, active citations, and cross-department collaboration metrics.
Manual Accreditation Prep

A purpose-built, secure academic collaboration suite with dedicated workflows for every tier of the research hierarchy.

🎓
Research Scholar
End-to-end doctoral workspace. Milestone tracking, report submissions, literature discovery, and direct supervisor communication.
  • Doctoral Milestone Roadmap
  • Research Workspace & Files
  • Nexus Collaboration Hub
  • Verified Publication Portfolio
👨‍🏫
Research Supervisor
Centralized supervisory command. Supervise assigned scholars, review doctoral reports, approve requests, and manage shared lab workspaces.

  • Quarterly Report Reviews

  • Department Research Analytics
🏛️
Institute Admin
Institutional governance. Campus & department hierarchies, user provisioning, academic integrity moderation, and platform audit logs.

  • Tamper-Proof Audit Logging
03 · Cross-Role Workflow

End-to-End Supervision Lifecycle

Structured, transparent, and auditable supervision milestones that keep scholars and supervisors aligned.

1

Scholar Submits Report

Scholar uploads empirical benchmarks and progress documentation under /my-research.

➔
2

Instant Notification

Supervisor receives high-priority notification with deep-link to the report review modal.

➔
3

Supervisor Reviews

Supervisor assesses findings, writes qualitative feedback, and approves or requests edits.

➔
4

Institutional Sync

Approved milestone logs into institutional database; leadership metrics update dynamically.

Zero Data Leakage & Zero Unauthorized Actions
Enforced simultaneously at Edge Middleware and NestJS API Guards (ApprovedGuard, SupervisorGuard, ScholarGuard).
100% RBAC Enforced

06 · Engineering Architecture

Enterprise Full-Stack Architecture

Built with enterprise reliability, high-performance querying, and zero compromise on institutional security.

Frontend Layer apps/web
  • Next.js 15 (App Router) + React 19 for optimal SSR/CSR performance
  • Tailwind CSS & Design Tokens with SRMIST visual identity
  • Zustand Store for reactive, optimized local state
  • Lucide Modern Icons with full responsive mobile & tablet adaptation
Backend & Database Layer apps/api & db
  • NestJS 11 Framework with modular, dependency-injected services
  • PostgreSQL + Prisma ORM (38 models, full relational indexing)
  • Supabase Auth + Google OAuth with strict institutional domain policy
  • Google Workspace & Zoom Workplace dynamic OAuth micro-services
